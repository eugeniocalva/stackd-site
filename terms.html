  5. Bank Connect (optional)

    Bank Connect lets you link a bank account and fetch its booked transactions instead of importing a file. It is off until you turn it on, and it relies on two third parties: Enable Banking Oy (Espoo, Finland), an account information service provider registered with and supervised by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority, which handles the connection to your bank; and the Stack'd server, which relays the data to your device without storing it. You give consent on your bank's own pages, never inside Stack'd, and your bank credentials never pass through the app or the server. Access expires after the period your bank allows (usually 90 to 180 days) and must then be renewed; banks also limit how often data can be refreshed. Every fetch is shown to you for review before anything is saved. Availability depends on your bank and on the provider and is not guaranteed. You may disconnect a bank at any time from the app, which revokes the access.

Refunds are handled by the store under its own rules. When a subscription ends, refreshing stops at once; your bank connections are kept for 14 days so a renewal picks up where you left off, and are then revoked. Transactions already imported stay in the app. Apart from Bank Connect and the optional Stack'd Pro unlock described next, every feature of Stack'd is free.

Stack'd Pro is an optional one-time purchase that lifts both limits for good: as many wallets as you need, and categories of your own. It is billed once by Apple or Google through your store account at the price shown in the app; it is not a subscription and never renews. You can restore it at any time on any device signed in to the same store account. Refunds are handled by the store under its own rules. Data you already have is never hidden or deleted, and only creating more is limited on the free plan.
